# Data Retention Policy

관련 / Keywords: data retention disposal records lifecycle

본 문서는 Data Retention Policy 에 관한 사내 기준을 정의한다.

- Each data category has a defined retention period.
- Records are disposed of securely once the retention period ends.
- Legal hold suspends disposal for data under investigation.
- Retention schedules are reviewed annually for compliance.
- Disposal of personal data follows the privacy destruction procedure.

Data retention aligns with the records preservation and privacy rules.
