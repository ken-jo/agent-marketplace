# Backup and Disaster Recovery

관련 / Keywords: 데이터 백업 복구

This policy defines how critical data is backed up and recovered.

- Core business data is backed up automatically every day.
- Backups are stored redundantly at a remote site.
- Recovery drills are conducted every quarter to validate the backups.
- Backup media is encrypted at rest.
- Backup retention periods depend on the importance of the data.

Recovery time objectives are defined per system in the recovery plan.
