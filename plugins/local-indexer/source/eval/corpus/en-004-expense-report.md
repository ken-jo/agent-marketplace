# Expense Reimbursement Report

관련 / Keywords: 경비 정산 품의

This document describes how to submit and reimburse business expenses.

- Obtain pre-approval before incurring significant business expenses.
- Submit the expense report with original receipts within one week of spending.
- Expenses above a set threshold require dual approval from the manager and finance.
- Entertainment expenses follow a separate limit and documentation standard.
- Fraudulent expense claims are subject to disciplinary action.

Reimbursements are paid out with the next scheduled payroll cycle.
