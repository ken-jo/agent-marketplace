# Vendor Risk Assessment

관련 / Keywords: vendor risk assessment third party due diligence

본 문서는 Vendor Risk Assessment 에 관한 사내 기준을 정의한다.

- New vendors undergo a security and financial risk assessment.
- High-risk vendors require additional due diligence and controls.
- Vendor access to systems follows least-privilege principles.
- Assessments are repeated periodically for active vendors.
- Findings feed into the vendor evaluation and onboarding decision.

Vendor risk results inform procurement and contract decisions.
