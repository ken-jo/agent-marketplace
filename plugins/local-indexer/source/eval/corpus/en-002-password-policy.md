# Password Policy

관련 / Keywords: 비밀번호 정책

All employee account passwords must follow these requirements.

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
- Passwords must combine uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Recently used passwords may not be reused.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for access to critical systems.

Passwords must never be shared or written down in plain text.
