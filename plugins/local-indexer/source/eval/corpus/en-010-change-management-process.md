# Change Management Process

관련 / Keywords: change management release approval rollback

본 문서는 Change Management Process 에 관한 사내 기준을 정의한다.

- System changes follow a request, review, and approval workflow.
- Each change records its risk level and rollback plan.
- Changes are scheduled in a maintenance window when possible.
- Emergency changes are documented and reviewed after the fact.
- Change outcomes are verified before the ticket is closed.

Change management connects deployment, monitoring, and incident response.
