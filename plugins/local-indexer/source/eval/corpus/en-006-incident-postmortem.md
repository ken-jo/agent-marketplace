# Incident Postmortem Guideline

관련 / Keywords: postmortem incident review root cause

본 문서는 Incident Postmortem Guideline 에 관한 사내 기준을 정의한다.

- After a major incident, the team writes a blameless postmortem.
- The postmortem documents the timeline, impact, and root cause.
- Action items are assigned with owners and due dates.
- Postmortems are shared so the whole organization can learn.
- Recurring root causes trigger a deeper systemic review.

Postmortem records feed into the incident response improvement loop.
