# Employee Onboarding Guide

관련 / Keywords: 신규 입사 온보딩 채용

This guide helps new employees get started during their first weeks.

- New hires complete HR registration and receive their accounts on the first day.
- Each new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy and a manager check-in schedule.
- Mandatory training covers security, the code of conduct, and core tools.
- New hires set initial goals with their manager within the first month.
- Feedback on the onboarding experience is collected after thirty days.

Managers review onboarding progress at the end of the probation period.
